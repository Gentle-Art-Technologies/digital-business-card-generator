# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7, selected by the user.

## Users

Professionals and teams evaluating a digital business card for networking, referrals, and sharing current contact information.

## Product Purpose

Let an Alianza professional create, customize, preview, save, and share a mobile-first digital business card.

## Positioning

The product turns Alianza's trusted professional identity into an always-current, mobile-first contact card that recipients can open without installing an app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first release is an interactive card-building app modeled on Blinq's creator workflow, plus a public card view.
- The implementation must use Astro 7.
- The app stores draft card data locally in the browser; production authentication and backend persistence are not yet supplied.
- Company name, company website, and the Alianza nest mark are fixed brand data and are never profile-editable.
- Profiles accept LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook usernames; WhatsApp accepts a full international phone number and generates the corresponding profile links.
- A card can only be saved or shared with an `@alianza.health` email address.
- Person-specific names, testimonials, metrics, and company claims are demo content unless supplied by the user.

## Product Principles

- Show the product in use before explaining it.
- Make the primary action obvious and repeat it at meaningful decision points.
- Keep every claim honest and every demo identity clearly labeled.
- Make the page fast, keyboard-accessible, and usable across device sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard-first interactions, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and touch targets of at least 44px.
